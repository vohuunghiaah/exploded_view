# Lenovo LOQ 15IAX9 (Machine Type 83GS) — Hardware Technical Specifications

## 1. General Overview
- **Brand & Model:** Lenovo LOQ 15IAX9
- **Model Year:** 2024 (Gen 9)
- **Machine Type (MTM):** 83GS
- **Form Factor:** 15.6" Gaming Laptop
- **Chassis Dimensions:** 359.86 × 258.7 × 21.9 – 23.9 mm (14.17 × 10.19 × 0.86 – 0.94 in)
- **Weight:** Approx. 2.38 kg (5.25 lbs)
- **Color & Finish:** Luna Grey (anodized finish on PC-ABS polymer)

---

## 2. Display Subsystem
- **Screen Size:** 15.6 inches diagonal (16:9 aspect ratio)
- **Active Area:** 344.16 × 193.59 mm
- **Resolution Options:** 
  - FHD (1920 × 1080) IPS, 144Hz, 300 nits, 100% sRGB, G-SYNC support
  - WQHD (2560 × 1440) IPS, 165Hz, 350 nits, 100% sRGB, G-SYNC support
- **Hinge Mechanism:** Dual zinc-alloy reinforced drop hinges, opening angle up to 180°
- **Bezel Design:** 3-sided narrow bezel with top camera notch
- **Webcam:** 720p HD / 1080p FHD with electronic privacy shutter switch (E-Shutter toggle on right chassis) and dual array microphones

---

## 3. Processing & Core Architecture
- **CPU Platform:** Intel 12th Gen Core HX Architecture (Socket BGA1964)
  - Reference configuration: Intel Core i5-12450HX (8 cores: 4 P-cores + 4 E-cores, 12 threads, up to 4.4 GHz turbo, 12MB cache)
- **GPU Platform:** Dedicated NVIDIA GeForce RTX Laptop GPU
  - Reference configurations: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 6GB GDDR6 / RTX 4050 6GB GDDR6 / RTX 4060 8GB GDDR6
  - Dedicated GDDR6 VRAM chips surrounding the GPU die
- **Chipset:** Intel HM670 (Chính thức theo Lenovo PSREF LOQ 15IAX9)
- **AI Engine:** Lenovo LA1 AI chip for dynamic CPU/GPU thermal power balancing

---

## 4. Memory & Storage Subsystem
- **RAM Slots:** 2× DDR5 SO-DIMM 262-pin slots
  - Max supported: 32GB DDR5-4800 / DDR5-5600 dual channel
  - Protection: Removable stamped metal EMI shielding can with internal thermal pads
- **Storage Slots:** 2× M.2 PCIe 4.0 ×4 slots
  - Slot 1 (Primary): Supports M.2 2242 / 2280 NVMe SSD with heat sink bracket
  - Slot 2 (Secondary): Supports M.2 2280 NVMe SSD expansion
- **WLAN:** 1× M.2 2230 Key-E slot populated with Intel Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax 2×2) + Bluetooth 5.2 card and dual RF antenna cables

---

## 5. Thermal & Cooling Architecture (Lenovo Hyperchamber)
- **Thermal Design:** Lenovo Hyperchamber Thermal Architecture
- **Fans:** 2× ultra-thin multi-blade centrifugal blower fans
  - Left Fan: Dedicated primary CPU cooling blower
  - Right Fan: Dedicated primary GPU cooling blower
  - Air intake: Cold air drawn from bottom intake grilles
  - Air exhaust: High velocity hot air pushed rearwards through dual high-density copper fin radiators
- **Heat Pipes:** 
  - Thick shared D8 copper composite heat pipe bridging CPU and GPU
  - Dedicated CPU secondary heat pipe
  - Dedicated GPU secondary heat pipe with VRM/VRAM copper cold plates
- **Mounting & Sequence:** 5× captive spring-loaded screws stamped alphabetically A through E. Loosen in alphabetical sequence (A → B → C → D → E) during removal to relieve spring tension evenly; tighten in reverse alphabetical sequence (E → D → C → B → A) with specified torque $1.85 \pm 0.15\text{ kgf-cm}$ per Lenovo HMM.

---

## 6. Power & Battery Subsystem
- **Internal Battery:** 4-cell Lithium-ion Polymer pack (Model L23M4PC0 / L23C4PC0)
  - Capacity: 60 Wh (3910 mAh, 15.36V nominal)
  - Features: Rapid Charge Pro (0% to 50% in 30 minutes)
  - Fastening: 4× Phillips screws, front-center chassis mounting
- **Power Inlets:**
  - Lenovo Slim Tip DC connector (supports 135W / 170W / 230W power adapters)
  - Rear USB-C Power Delivery 3.0 up to 140W (20V @ 7A proprietary Lenovo profile)

---

## 7. External I/O & Enclosure Features
- **Rear Thermal Shelf:** Signature Legion/LOQ overhang behind display hinges:
  - 1× DC-in (Lenovo Slim Tip)
  - 1× RJ-45 Gigabit Ethernet port
  - 1× HDMI 2.1 (supports up to 8K @ 60Hz)
  - 2× USB 3.2 Gen 1 (USB-A 5Gbps)
  - 1× USB 3.2 Gen 2 (Type-C 10Gbps with DP 1.4 & 140W PD)
  - Dual rear exhaust ventilation ports
- **Left Side:**
  - 1× 3.5mm Headphone / microphone combo audio jack
- **Right Side:**
  - 1× USB 3.2 Gen 1 (USB-A 5Gbps)
  - 1× E-Shutter hardware privacy switch
- **Lower Case (D-cover):**
  - Geometric intake hexagonal grille with anti-dust mesh
  - 10× captive/removable Phillips screws of specified lengths
  - Full-width front rubber footing and dual elevated rear stability feet
